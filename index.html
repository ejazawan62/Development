Get connected

From request to Wi-Fi in about two days.

  1. Send a request

    Fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp with your address and package.

  2. Site survey

    A technician confirms coverage and the best cable or antenna route, usually the same day.

  3. Installation

    We install the line and router and test speed in front of you. Bring your CNIC copy.

  4. Go online

    Pay your first month and start browsing. Your invoice comes by SMS each month.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the data really unlimited?

Yes. There are no daily or monthly data caps and no speed reduction after a certain usage.

What documents do I need?

A copy of the account holder's CNIC and the installation address. Businesses also need an NTN or company letterhead for invoicing.

Will the internet work during load-shedding?

Our network nodes run on battery backup. Your router needs power too, so a small router UPS (about Rs 4,000) keeps you online through outages.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my package?

Yes, any time. The change takes effect within 24 hours and your next bill is adjusted.

How do I report a fault?

Call the helpline or send a WhatsApp message with your customer ID. Most faults are fixed remotely within an hour; a technician visit is booked if needed.
