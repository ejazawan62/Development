Four logo concepts for DPIS

All four share the same deep Pakistan green and an amber "signal" colour. Each is shown on white, on the dark brand green, and at favicon sizes to check it still reads when small. The website currently uses Signal D.

Recommended

1. Signal D

The letter D drawn as a Wi-Fi signal. Simple, works in one colour, and stays clear as a favicon, app icon or on a router sticker.

32 / 16px

2. Crescent Link

The crescent from the national flag, with the star replaced by three connected network nodes. Strongly Pakistani and patriotic in feel.

32 / 16px

3. Fiber Tile

An app-icon style tile with three strands of fiber-optic light. Modern, and ready to use as a WhatsApp or Facebook profile picture.

64px wide

4. Tower Wordmark

The full name DPIS with the letter I turned into a broadcast tower. Best for signboards, vehicles and letterheads; too detailed for a favicon.
